Project Overview

St Michaels Street is being converted into a six-bedroom HMO, with every bedroom having its own ensuite bathroom.

We do not cut corners on HMO projects. The spec here is high throughout, covering quality fixtures and fittings, good sound insulation between rooms, and communal spaces that are actually pleasant to use. The goal is shared accommodation that tenants genuinely appreciate living in, not just somewhere that ticks the minimum boxes. The full renovation is being carried out in house by our team.
