Gloucester's Central Post Office

Project Overview

A landmark development at Kings Square, converting the upper floors of the historic Georgian Central Post Office building into 18 luxury residential units whilst preserving the architectural heritage of this iconic Gloucester building.

This is one of the most significant projects in our current pipeline. Bringing a prominent listed building back into productive use takes careful planning, close work with the local authority, and a genuine respect for the fabric of the original structure. We are approaching it exactly the way we approach every project: in house, end to end, and to a standard we are proud to put our name to.
