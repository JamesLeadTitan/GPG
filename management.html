Management

We manage our own portfolio of HMOs, apartment blocks and supported accommodation in house.

We do not hand our buildings to a letting agent and hope for the best. We manage them ourselves, from finding and looking after tenants to maintenance, compliance and the day to day running of every property we hold.

That includes specialist and supported accommodation, where getting the details right matters more than anywhere else. We work with providers on long-term arrangements built on trust, and we hold ourselves to the standard those tenancies require.

Because we own, renovate and manage everything under one roof, there is no chain of third parties to slow things down. If something needs doing, we deal with it.
