Investments

We buy run-down and undervalued property across Gloucester and Cheltenham, and bring it back into productive use.

Most of what we buy comes off-market, through agents who know we complete quickly and do not pull out, or through lease options and creative deals that suit both sides. We have bought blocks that were empty and derelict, buildings other developers would not touch, and properties where the numbers only worked because we did the due diligence properly first.

Every deal is underwritten on real figures before we commit: purchase price, realistic renovation cost, end value and rent. We do not chase headline prices or overpay to win a deal. We would rather walk away than take on something that does not stack up.

That discipline is how we have built a portfolio of 49 assets across the city, and it is how we intend to keep growing it.
