Construction

Our own team carries out the full build on every project, from structural work through to the final finish.

Every renovation and conversion we take on is delivered by our own team. Structural work, extensions, groundwork, first and second fix, and the interior finish are all handled in house, on one contract, to one standard.

We brought Grade II listed buildings back to life, dug basements, converted lofts and reconfigured blocks of flats, and we have dealt with the problems those jobs throw up: rotten joists, buildings that needed re-supporting, planning and heritage requirements that had to be worked through properly. We do not cut corners to protect a margin, and we do not walk away when something unexpected turns up.

That same capability is available on select private commissions for clients who want a job done properly, start to finish, by the team that will actually be on site.
