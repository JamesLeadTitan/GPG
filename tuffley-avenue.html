Project Overview

Tuffley Avenue is a large detached bungalow that we are converting into a four-bedroom property for a long-term client in the care sector.

The property is being fully stripped back and rebuilt to the client's exact requirements, with layouts, room sizes, and finishes all tailored to suit the specific needs of the people who will be living there. It is the kind of project where getting the details right really matters, and we are working closely with the client throughout to make sure the finished property does exactly what it needs to. All works are being carried out by our own team.
